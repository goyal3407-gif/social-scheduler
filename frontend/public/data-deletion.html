How to delete your data

KiluaAI — operated by Goyal Kumar.
This page is the data deletion instructions URL for our Meta app integration.

Option 1 — Delete your account in the app (immediate)

  1. Sign in at social-scheduler-one-sandy.vercel.app.
  2. Open Settings and choose Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. This permanently removes your user account, login sessions, scheduled and published post records, uploaded media records, templates, settings, and your stored Instagram access token from our database.

Option 2 — Remove only your Instagram data

  1. In the app, open Settings and disconnect Instagram — this deletes the stored Instagram access token, user ID and username.
  2. You can also revoke access from Instagram/Facebook directly: Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites → remove KiluaAI.

Option 3 — Ask us by email

If you cannot sign in, email goyaldhiman00@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject “Data deletion request”. We will delete your account and all associated data and confirm by email within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What gets deleted

  • Account: email address, password hash, sessions.
  • Instagram connection: access token (stored encrypted), Instagram user ID, username and cached profile.
  • Content: scheduled/published post records, media records, templates and settings.

Posts already published to Instagram itself are controlled by Instagram — deleting your Scheduler account does not remove posts from your Instagram profile; delete those in the Instagram app if you want them gone.
