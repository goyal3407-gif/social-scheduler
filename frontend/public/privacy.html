Privacy Policy

Goyal Kumar (Social Scheduler) — operated by Goyal Kumar.
Effective date: 17 November 2026. Contact: goyaldhiman00@gmail.com

Goyal Kumar is a tool that lets you schedule and publish posts to your own Instagram account. This policy explains exactly what data the service stores and why.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a hashed password (we never store your password in plain text), plus login session tokens.
  • Instagram connection data: if you connect Instagram, the Instagram access token you provide, your Instagram user ID and username. Access tokens are stored encrypted in our database and are never shown in full in the app.
  • Your content: posts you create or schedule (captions, schedule times, publish status), media you upload (photos and videos), and reusable templates and settings you save.
  • Technical data: basic request logs (method, path, response status and timing). Logs never contain passwords, tokens or post contents.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To publish your scheduled posts to Instagram on your behalf, using the official Meta/Instagram APIs, at the times you choose.
  • To show you your queue, history and connected account status.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or post anything to your Instagram account that you did not schedule.

Who processes it

  • Meta / Instagram — receives your media and captions when a scheduled post is published, under Meta's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Vercel — hosts the application.
  • MongoDB Atlas — stores the database described above.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your user record, sessions, posts, media records, templates, settings and stored Instagram token are removed (see the data deletion page).

Your choices

  • Disconnect Instagram at any time in Settings — this removes the stored token from our database. You can also revoke the app's access from your Instagram/Facebook settings.
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time in Settings, or by following the instructions on the data deletion page.
  • Ask us what data we hold about you by emailing goyaldhiman00@gmail.com.

Children

This service is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
